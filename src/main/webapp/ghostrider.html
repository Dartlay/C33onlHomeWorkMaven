Ghost Rider

Ghost Rider is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character is a supernatural antihero who rides a fiery motorcycle and is covered in flames. The Ghost Rider is usually depicted as a human with a flaming skull for a head.

The most well-known version of Ghost Rider is Johnny Blaze, a stunt motorcyclist who sells his soul to the demon Mephisto to save his adoptive father from cancer. Blaze is transformed into the Ghost Rider at night or when in the presence of evil.

Ghost Rider possesses superhuman strength, durability, and the ability to project hellfire. His signature weapons are his mystical chains and his Penance Stare, which makes wrongdoers feel all the pain they've inflicted on others.
